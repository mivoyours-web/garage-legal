Support

Passbook app · your car's complete history

Contact

Write to carpassbook@gmail.com. Passbook has no server and collects nothing, so the developer cannot see your data — when you write, please say which iPhone and iOS version you use and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help.

My cars do not sync between devices

Passbook syncs through your own iCloud. Check, on every device, that you are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is on for Passbook (Settings → your name → iCloud → Saved to iCloud). In Passbook, the iCloud & Data screen in Settings shows the last sync and any error. Syncing needs a network connection; changes wait until one is available.

I paid for Premium but the app says I have not

Open Settings → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases. This asks the App Store for your subscription or Lifetime purchase; it needs a network connection. If you purchased under a different Apple Account, sign in with that one first. Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.

What is free and what is Premium?

One car is always free and fully functional — records, deadlines, maintenance plan, VIN scanning, safety recalls, fuel, documents, reminders, Calendar & Reminders sync, export and statistics. Premium (Monthly, Yearly, or one-time Lifetime unlock; Offer Codes supported) unlocks every car after the first, exporting extra cars, and sharing a car with another person via iCloud. If a subscription ends, nothing is deleted or hidden: everything you entered stays readable, and your first car keeps working in full.

The sample car

Passbook can show a sample car so you can see what the app does before you enter anything. It is marked "Sample". On the device that added it, it disappears by itself after three days unless you have added anything to it; on any other device it stays until you remove it. You can remove it at any time from the car's ⋯ menu (Remove Sample Car).

Location on records and fill-ups

Location access is completely optional. Passbook never asks for location on launch or tracks you in the background. Only when you tap Use Current Location while adding or editing a service record or fuel fill-up does iOS ask for permission to read your current coordinates once and suggest a place name. You can edit or clear a saved location at any time.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

Passbook updates widget snapshots locally on your device whenever your cars, deadlines or fuel entries change. In Widget settings on your Home Screen you can configure which car a widget follows.

Getting my data out

Your free car can always be exported as CSV or PDF (Car History or Vehicle Passport) from its Export screen; with Premium, every car can. All vehicles in Passbook can be backed up and restored as a JSON file (with photos and saved locations) from Settings → Data. Your data is yours at all times, and nothing you entered is ever hidden or deleted when a subscription ends.

Deleting my data

Settings → Data → Delete All Data removes every car, record, photo and document from the app. If iCloud sync is on, it is also deleted from your iCloud and from every other device signed in to the same Apple Account. There is no account to delete because Passbook has no accounts.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. In short: no server, no analytics, no tracking — the developer never receives your data.
