Support

Garage app · your car's complete history

Contact

Write to mivo.yours@gmail.com. Garage has no server and collects nothing, so the developer cannot see your data — when you write, please say which iPhone and iOS version you use and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help.

My cars do not sync between devices

Garage syncs through your own iCloud. Check, on every device, that you are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is on for Garage (Settings → your name → iCloud → Saved to iCloud). In Garage, the iCloud & Data screen in Settings shows the last sync and any error. Syncing needs a network connection; changes wait until one is available.

I paid for Premium but the app says I have not

Open Settings → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases. This asks the App Store for your subscription; it needs a network connection. If you subscribed under a different Apple Account, sign in with that one first. Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

What is free and what is Premium?

One car is always free and fully functional — records, deadlines, fuel, documents, reminders, export, statistics. Premium unlocks every car after the first, and sharing a car with another person. If a subscription ends, nothing is deleted or hidden: everything you entered stays readable, and your first car keeps working in full.

The sample car

Garage can show a sample car so you can see what the app does before you enter anything. It is marked "Sample". On the device that added it, it disappears by itself after three days unless you have added anything to it; on any other device it stays until you remove it. You can remove it at any time from the car's ⋯ menu (Remove Sample Car).

Getting my data out

Your free car can always be exported as CSV or PDF from its Export screen; with Premium, every car can. The whole garage can be backed up as a JSON file (with photos) from Settings → Data. Your data is yours at all times, and nothing you entered is ever hidden or deleted when a subscription ends.

Deleting my data

Settings → Data → Delete All Data removes every car, record, photo and document from the app. If iCloud sync is on, it is also deleted from your iCloud and from every other device signed in to the same Apple Account. There is no account to delete because Garage has no accounts.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. In short: no server, no analytics, no tracking — the developer never receives your data.
