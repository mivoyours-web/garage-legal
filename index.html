Privacy Policy Garage app · effective 17 August 2026 In short We do not collect your data. Garage has no server. Everything you enter about your cars — records, deadlines, photos, documents, costs — stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. The developer has no access to it and receives no copy. What data is processed, and where it lives The app works with the data you enter yourself: vehicles, service records, deadlines (insurance, inspection, vignette, tax), fuel fill-ups, documents and attached photos, costs and amounts. This data is stored: locally on your device; if you are signed in to iCloud — in the private database of your iCloud account, via Apple's CloudKit. Syncing happens between your own devices. The app does not transmit this data to the developer or to any third party. Sign in with Apple Signing in with Apple is optional. The app works fully without it and no feature requires an account. If you do sign in, only your name is used, to greet you. It stays on your device and is not sent anywhere. Sharing a vehicle If you share a vehicle with someone, the sharing runs entirely through Apple's iCloud sharing mechanism. You choose who and with what permissions. The developer is not involved and cannot see the shared data. Permissions the app requests Photos — to attach a photo of a vehicle, invoice or document. Access is limited to what you select. Camera — to photograph a fuel receipt and read the litres and total. Recognition happens on the device itself. Notifications — reminders before a deadline expires. Notifications are scheduled locally on the device. Face ID / Touch ID — only if you turn on the app lock yourself. Biometrics are handled by iOS; the app receives only a yes or no. Analytics, advertising and tracking None. The app contains no ad networks, analytics systems or tracking tools, and builds no profile of you. Subscription and payments Garage offers an optional subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees or stores card or payment details. One vehicle is free and fully functional without any subscription. If a subscription ends, you keep access to everything you have already entered — your history remains readable, and your first vehicle continues to work in full. Your rights Because the data lives in your iCloud account and on your device, you control it directly: You can delete everything from the app's Settings. You can delete the app — local data goes with it. iCloud data is managed from your Apple Account settings. Because the developer stores none of your data, there is nothing for the developer to provide or erase on request. Children The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them. Changes If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above changes.
