Privacy Policy

Passbook app · effective 24 November 2026

In short

We do not collect your data. Passbook has no developer server. Everything you enter about your cars — records, deadlines, photos, documents, costs and optional locations — stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. The developer has no access to it and receives no copy.

What data is processed, and where it lives

The app works with the data you enter yourself: vehicles, service records, deadlines (insurance, inspection, vignette, tax), fuel fill-ups, documents and attached photos, costs and amounts, and — only when you choose to add them — optional place names and coordinates on service records or fill-ups.

This data is stored:

  • locally on your device (including a compact on-device App Group snapshot so Passbook's Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets can display your car data);
  • if you are signed in to iCloud — in the private database of your iCloud account, via Apple's CloudKit. Syncing happens between your own devices.

The app does not transmit your personal data to the developer or to any advertising or analytics third party.

Optional third-party public lookups

  • VIN decoding & U.S. Safety Recalls (NHTSA) — only when you explicitly choose to decode a VIN or check Safety Recalls, Passbook queries the public U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration API (vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov and api.nhtsa.gov) using the VIN or your vehicle's make, model and year. No account, device identifier or other personal record is included in those requests.
  • Currency exchange rates (Frankfurter / ECB) — if you log expenses in multiple currencies, Passbook refreshes anonymous daily European Central Bank reference rates from api.frankfurter.app at most once every 24 hours. No user or vehicle information is ever sent.

Accounts

Passbook has no accounts. Nothing asks you to sign in, register or give an email address; your iCloud account, managed by Apple, is the only identity involved, and the developer never sees it.

Sharing a vehicle

If you share a vehicle with someone, the sharing runs entirely through Apple's iCloud sharing mechanism. You choose who and with what permissions. The developer is not involved and cannot see the shared data.

Permissions the app requests

  • Photos — to attach a photo of a vehicle, invoice or document. Access is limited to what you select.
  • Camera — to photograph fuel receipts, VIN plates, documents, policies and invoices. Text, barcode and QR recognition happens on the device itself.
  • Calendar & Reminders (optional) — requested only if you turn on Apple Calendar or Apple Reminders sync in Settings. Passbook writes and updates your car deadlines directly in your device's Calendar or Reminders through Apple EventKit.
  • Location (optional, When In Use) — requested only if you explicitly tap Use Current Location on a service record or fuel fill-up. Passbook never tracks your location automatically or in the background. Coordinates are looked up once; iOS MapKit / CoreLocation reverse-geocoding may suggest a readable place name, and the saved coordinates stay on your device and in your iCloud (never on developer servers). Tapping Open in Maps opens Apple Maps with those coordinates.
  • Notifications — reminders before a deadline expires. Notifications are scheduled locally on the device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — only if you turn on the app lock yourself. Biometrics are handled by iOS; the app receives only a yes or no.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. The app contains no ad networks, analytics systems or tracking tools, and builds no profile of you.

Subscription and payments

Passbook offers optional Premium plans — a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase — as well as App Store Offer Codes. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees or stores card or payment details. One vehicle is free and fully functional without any purchase.

If a subscription ends, you keep access to everything you have already entered — your history remains readable, and your first vehicle continues to work in full.

Your rights

Because the data lives in your iCloud account and on your device, you control it directly:

  • You can delete everything from the app's Settings.
  • You can delete the app — local data goes with it.
  • iCloud data is managed from your Apple Account settings.

Because the developer stores none of your data, there is nothing for the developer to provide or erase on request.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.
